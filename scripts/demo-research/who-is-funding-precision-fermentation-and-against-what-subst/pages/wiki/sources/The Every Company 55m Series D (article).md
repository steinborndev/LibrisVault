# The Every Company 55m Series D (article)

AgFunderNews report on The Every Company's late-2025 Series D for its precision-fermented
egg proteins, cross-checked against parallel coverage (Green Queen, FoodNavigator-USA) for the
investor list and cumulative funding total.

URL: https://agfundernews.com/the-every-co-raises-55m-to-scale-egg-proteins-via-fermentation-crosses-line-from-promise-to-proof-says-investor

## Summary

The Every Company raised $55 million led by existing investor McWin Capital Partners, with
Main Sequence, Bloom8, TO.VC, Minerva Foods, Grosvenor Food & Ag, New Agrarian and SOSV
participating, bringing cumulative funding to roughly $290-294 million. An investor is quoted
describing the round as the company crossing "from promise to proof" - retail distribution at
Walmart alongside the raise is offered as the evidence for that claim.

## Caveats

- Cumulative funding is reported inconsistently across outlets covering the same round
  ($290 million vs. $294 million); both figures are given rather than resolved.
- The article's own headline framing ("crosses line from promise to proof") is an investor's
  quoted characterization, not an independently verified commercial milestone.
