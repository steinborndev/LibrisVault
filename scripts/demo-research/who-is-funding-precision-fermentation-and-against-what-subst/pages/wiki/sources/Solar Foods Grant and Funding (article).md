# Solar Foods Grant and Funding (article)

AgFunderNews coverage of Solar Foods' funding history, combining an EUR10 million Business
Finland/IPCEI grant announcement with the company's earlier retail-investor equity round.

URL: https://agfundernews.com/gas-fermentation-startup-solar-foods-bags-10-6m-grant-to-enter-pre-engineering-phase-of-commercial-scale-plant

## Summary

Solar Foods secured a EUR10 million ($10.6 million) non-dilutive grant from Business Finland,
under the European Commission's Important Projects of Common European Interest programme, to
fund the pre-engineering phase of a commercial-scale plant. This sits alongside an earlier
EUR8 million ($8.8 million) retail-investor equity round via Springvest Oyj, for cumulative
reported funding of roughly $38 million across about ten rounds. The company's process feeds
its bacteria CO2 and water, with hydrogen supplied by electrolysis, making it feedstock-free of
any agricultural or food-industry input.

## Caveats

- The IPCEI grant is tied to a specific project phase (pre-engineering), not general
  operating capital, and should not be summed with equity rounds as if fungible.
- "Roughly $38 million across ten rounds" is a third-party (Seedtable) aggregate figure not
  independently itemized in this article.
