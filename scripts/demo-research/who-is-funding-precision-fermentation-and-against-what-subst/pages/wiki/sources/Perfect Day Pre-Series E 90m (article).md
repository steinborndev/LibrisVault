# Perfect Day Pre-Series E 90m (article)

vegconomist report on Perfect Day's pre-Series E round and simultaneous co-founder leadership
transition, cross-referenced with AgFunderNews coverage of the company's Gujarat, India
production facility timeline.

URL: https://vegconomist.com/investments-finance/perfect-day-90m-whey-proteins-co-founders-step-dow/

## Summary

Perfect Day raised a pre-Series E round of up to $90 million from existing investors, taking
cumulative funding past $825 million since its 2014 founding. Co-founders and former CEO Ryan
Pandya and Perumal Gandhi stepped down from operational roles at the same time, with
president TM Narayan named interim CEO. Separately, AgFunderNews reports the company's Gujarat
facility is on track for mechanical completion in September 2026, first commercial truckloads
in Q4 2026, and a full ramp-up in 2027.

- Supports [[Perfect Day]].

## Caveats

- The round being funded by "existing investors" only, rather than new backers, is reported
  without further detail on whether that reflects investor confidence or difficulty attracting
  new capital; both readings are consistent with the facts given.
- Perfect Day declined to comment on future fundraising plans, so nothing here indicates
  whether a full Series E is expected next.
