# Standing Ovation Raises 34m Series B (article)

AgFunderNews report on Standing Ovation's March 2026 Series B, covering the funding
structure, investor syndicate, feedstock choice and regulatory timeline for its
precision-fermented casein proteins.

URL: https://agfundernews.com/standing-ovation-nets-34m-gears-up-for-us-launch-of-casein-via-precision-fermentation

## Summary

Standing Ovation closed EUR30 million (EUR25m equity + EUR5m non-dilutive) to scale
production of three casein proteins ahead of a planned 2026 US launch. The equity tranche was
co-led by a French state development-bank vehicle (Bpifrance's Ecotechnologies 2 fund) and a
retail bank's innovation arm (Crédit Mutuel Innovation), alongside a mix of dedicated
food-tech VCs and a corporate strategic (Bel Group, a major cheese producer).
