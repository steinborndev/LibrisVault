# Arkeon Postmortem (article)

AgFunderNews interview with Arkeon co-founder and CEO Dr. Gregor Tegl following the Vienna
gas-fermentation startup's 2026 insolvency filing.

URL: https://agfundernews.com/arkeon-ceo-delivers-postmortem-on-protein-from-air-startup-we-just-simply-ran-out-of-time

## Summary

Tegl attributes Arkeon's shutdown to timing rather than the underlying CO2/hydrogen
gas-fermentation technology: a 2024 fundraise that started strong lost momentum when a major
committed investor withdrew unexpectedly, a strategic pivot from mixed amino acids to a single
amino acid disrupted existing industry-partner agreements, and expected commercial deals did
not close in time to bridge the gap. He maintains the process could match incumbent amino-acid
production on price at scale, but concedes investors view gas fermentation as carrying
materially higher risk than sugar- or side-stream-fed processes.
