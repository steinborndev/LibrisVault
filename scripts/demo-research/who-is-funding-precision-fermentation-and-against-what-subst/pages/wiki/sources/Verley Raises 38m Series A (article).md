# Verley Raises 38m Series A (article)

AgFunderNews report on Verley's February 2026 Series A for its fungal-fermented
beta-lactoglobulin (BLG) whey protein.

URL: https://agfundernews.com/precision-fermentation-startup-verley-raises-38m-series-a-as-blg-unlocks-new-segment-of-protein-market

## Summary

Verley raised EUR32 million (EUR25m equity from a syndicate of French VCs plus EUR7m
non-dilutive from Bpifrance) to scale its *Aspergillus oryzae*-based BLG production ahead of a
planned US entry. Management is quoted framing unit-economics improvement as coming from both
internal R&D progress and external tailwinds (rising conventional whey prices), which
narrows - without yet closing - the gap to price parity.

## Extracted claims

- Supports [[Verley]].
- Supports [[Precision Fermentation]] (fungal host, whey-protein product category).
- Partially supports [[Fermentation Feedstock Economics]] (price-parity dynamics); the
  specific feedstock used was not disclosed in this article.

## Caveats

- The article does not name Verley's actual growth substrate/feedstock, only the host
  organism (*Aspergillus oryzae*) and the product (BLG).
- "Unit economics improving" and "price parity getting easier" are direct paraphrases of
  company statements, not independently audited cost figures.
