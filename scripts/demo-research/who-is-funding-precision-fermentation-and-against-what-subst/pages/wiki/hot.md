## Key facts from this pass

- Fermentation-derived alt-protein funding fell from $632M (2024) to $357M (2025) per GFI, read only via a secondary summary and queued on [[Reading list]] for primary confirmation. Precision fermentation specifically still closed several rounds in that smaller pool: The Every Company ($55M, egg), Standing Ovation ($34.2M, casein), Verley ($38M, whey BLG).
- Substrate splits funded companies into a lower-risk tier (sugar, dairy/food-industry side-streams - Standing Ovation feeds acid whey + sugar) and a higher-risk tier (CO2/H2/methane gas fermentation - Solar Foods, Arkeon). Feedstock is 35-50% of COGS; downstream purification can be larger still (up to 85% for some biologics).
- Arkeon (CO2 + hydrogen "protein from air") raised ~$13M and still filed for insolvency in 2026 after a lead investor withdrew mid-raise - gas-fermentation's higher perceived risk overrode a funded company's own cost-parity case.
- French rounds (Standing Ovation, Verley) both feature Bpifrance as co-lead or material non-dilutive backer; US rounds lean on specialist VCs and existing investors instead - not confirmed as a real pattern vs. a two-country sample artifact.
