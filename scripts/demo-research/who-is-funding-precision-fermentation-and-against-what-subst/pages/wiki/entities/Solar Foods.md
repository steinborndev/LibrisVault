# Solar Foods

Helsinki-based gas-fermentation startup producing "Solein," a single-cell protein made by
bacteria fed captured CO2 and water, split into hydrogen and oxygen by electrolysis, rather
than any sugar or agricultural feedstock.

## Funding

Has raised a reported $38 million total across roughly ten rounds, including an EUR8 million
($8.8m) retail-investor round via Springvest Oyj, and an EUR10 million ($10.6 million) grant
from Business Finland under the European Commission's Important Projects of Common European
Interest (IPCEI) programme, earmarked for the pre-engineering phase of a commercial-scale
plant ([[Solar Foods Grant and Funding (article)]]).

## Substrate

CO2 and water, with hydrogen supplied via electrolysis - the same broad gas-fermentation
category as [[Arkeon]] and Calysta, but using direct air/industrial CO2 capture rather than
methane. This is the most feedstock-independent approach in the sector (no agricultural or
food-industry input at all), and correspondingly the highest-risk from a scale-up and cost
perspective; see [[Fermentation Feedstock Economics]].
