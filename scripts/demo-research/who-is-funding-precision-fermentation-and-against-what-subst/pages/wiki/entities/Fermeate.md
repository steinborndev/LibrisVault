# Fermeate

Industrial biotechnology startup selling an optogenetic (light-based) gene-expression control
system as a retrofit for existing fermentation infrastructure, rather than selling a
fermented product of its own.

## Funding

Closed a $2 million seed round in April 2026, led by Newfund Capital with SOSV, Ajinomoto
Group Ventures, Ki Tua Fund, Heuristic Capital Partners, Momentum Capital, Plug and Play,
Tesserakt Ventures and Ag Startup Engine participating
([[Fermeate Raises 2m Seed (article)]]). Ajinomoto Group Ventures' participation is notable:
Ajinomoto is itself a large-scale industrial fermentation producer (amino acids, seasonings),
making this a strategic as well as financial bet.

## What it sells against "substrate"

Fermeate does not change what a host organism is fed; it changes how gene expression is
switched on during the run, using light instead of a chemical inducer such as methanol. It
claims this lets customers switch feedstocks, or move from fed-batch to semi-continuous
processing, without new bioreactor hardware. Reported claims include a typical payback period
under 11 months and 60-300% output increases in partner collaborations, with the system
compatible with common industrial hosts including *Pichia pastoris*, and other conventional
and non-conventional yeast and bacteria species.
