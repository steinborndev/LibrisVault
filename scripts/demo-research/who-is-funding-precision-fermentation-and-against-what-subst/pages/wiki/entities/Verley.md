# Verley

French precision-fermentation startup producing beta-lactoglobulin (BLG), the main whey
protein in cow's milk, using an engineered *Aspergillus oryzae* fungal strain.

## Funding

Closed a EUR32 million ($38 million) Series A in February 2026: EUR25 million ($30 million)
in equity from Alven, Blast, Sofinnova, Captech Santé, Sparkfood and Founders Future, plus
EUR7 million ($8 million) in non-dilutive support from Bpifrance
([[Verley Raises 38m Series A (article)]]).

## Substrate and scale

The specific feedstock was not disclosed in reporting; the host is a fungal strain rather
than yeast or bacteria, which is a less common choice among funded precision-fermentation
dairy players. Production is scaling from a 50,000-litre to a 150+ cubic-metre fermenter.

## Stage and product line

Holds an FDA GRAS letter and is targeting US market entry in Q3-Q4 2026. Sells BLG in three
formulations: Native 100 BLG, FermWhey MicroStab and FermWhey Gel. Management describes unit
economics as improving faster than plan, crediting both R&D progress and rising conventional
whey prices for narrowing the gap to price parity.
