# The Every Company

San Francisco-based precision-fermentation startup producing recombinant egg proteins for use
in baked goods, pasta, protein beverages and coffee syrups, positioned against the $270 billion
global egg market.

## Funding

Closed a $55 million Series D in late 2025, led by existing investor McWin Capital Partners
(via its McWin Food Tech Fund), with Main Sequence, Bloom8, TO.VC, Minerva Foods, Grosvenor
Food & Ag, New Agrarian and SOSV participating. The round brought cumulative funding to
roughly $290-294 million (sources vary slightly on the exact cumulative figure)
([[The Every Company 55m Series D (article)]]).

## Stage

Proceeds are earmarked for expanding manufacturing capacity and moving toward profitability.
Its egg protein has reached retail distribution at Walmart, and an investor characterized the
round as the company crossing the line "from promise to proof" - the same phrase GFI used for
the sector's 2025 funding contraction generally (see
[[GFI Fermentation Funding Dip 2025 (article)]]).
