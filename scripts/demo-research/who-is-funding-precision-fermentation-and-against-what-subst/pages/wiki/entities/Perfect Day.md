# Perfect Day

US-based precision-fermentation pioneer, founded 2014, producing recombinant whey proteins
(marketed as ProFerm, fermented beta-lactoglobulin) using engineered fungal hosts. One of the
longest-running and most heavily capitalised companies in the sector, and a bellwether for the
industry more broadly.

## Funding

Has raised more than $825 million cumulatively since founding. Most recently closed a
pre-Series E round of up to $90 million led by existing investors, alongside a leadership
transition: co-founders and former CEO Ryan Pandya and Perumal Gandhi stepped down from
operational roles, with president TM Narayan becoming interim CEO
([[Perfect Day Pre-Series E 90m (article)]]). The company declined to comment on future
fundraising plans beyond describing continuous review of its capital structure.

## Scale

Building a production facility in Gujarat, India, targeting mechanical completion in
September 2026 and first commercial truckloads of product in Q4 2026, with a full ramp-up
expected in 2027.

## Why it matters to the funding picture

Perfect Day's scale of total capital raised (over $825M, an order of magnitude above most
peers in this survey) and its leadership change alongside a comparatively modest pre-Series E
round illustrate a maturing-sector pattern also visible at Arkeon and Motif FoodWorks: early,
large capital raises do not guarantee a smooth path to commercial scale, and later rounds in
this sector increasingly come from existing rather than new investors.
