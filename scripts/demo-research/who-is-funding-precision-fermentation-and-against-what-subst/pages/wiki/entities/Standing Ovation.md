# Standing Ovation

Paris-based precision-fermentation startup producing dairy casein proteins (alpha-s1,
alpha-s2 and beta-casein) without animals.

## Funding

Closed a EUR30 million ($34.2 million) round in March 2026: EUR25 million ($28.5 million) in
equity plus EUR5 million ($5.7 million) non-dilutive. The equity tranche was jointly led by
the Ecotechnologies 2 fund (managed by Bpifrance on behalf of the French government under the
France 2030 initiative) and Crédit Mutuel Innovation, with participation from Astanor
Ventures, Bel Group, Seventure Partners, GoodStartUp, Danone Ventures, Big Idea Ventures,
Angelor, Newtree and Noshaq
([[Standing Ovation Raises 34m Series B (article)]]).

The round is a case of a French state development-bank vehicle and a domestic bank co-leading
ahead of - not alongside - the specialist food-tech and dairy-strategic investors, which is
unusual for the sector.

## Substrate

Feeds its host organism acid whey, a byproduct of cottage cheese and Greek yogurt production,
supplemented with sugar in an undisclosed ratio. The company frames this as a deliberate
choice: "it's super important for us not to buy food to build food." See
[[Fermentation Feedstock Economics]].

## Stage

Targeting a US launch in 2026 (seeking an FDA "no questions" letter by end of 2026) and
European/Asian expansion in late 2027 pending an EFSA novel-foods approval.
