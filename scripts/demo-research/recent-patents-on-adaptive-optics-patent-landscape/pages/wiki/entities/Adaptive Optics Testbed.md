# Adaptive Optics Testbed

Appears throughout the astronomy material as the thing that produced, holds or standardises
the data other pages argue about.

## Role

Pages cite it when the provenance of a number matters: what was measured, under which
programme, and which release the figure came from.

## What it constrains

Its coverage and cadence set what questions can be asked at all. A gap in the record is not
a null result, and treating it as one is the most common way conclusions drift from what the
data can support.

## Predictive control on the bench

This is the generic role real on-sky demonstrations fill before a predictive-control scheme is trusted with instrument time: PO4AO was proven on the 1.52 m telescope at Observatoire de Haute-Provence before reinforcement-learning control was considered for a larger instrument, and empirical-orthogonal-function prediction ran on Subaru/SCExAO and the Keck II near-infrared pyramid wavefront sensor before appearing in reviews. See [[Predictive Control (Adaptive Optics)]] for what was actually tested where.
