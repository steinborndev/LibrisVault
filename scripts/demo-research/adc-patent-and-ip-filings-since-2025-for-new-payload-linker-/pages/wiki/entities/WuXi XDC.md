# WuXi XDC

A bioconjugate contract research, development and manufacturing organisation (CRDMO) - part of the WuXi group - that both files its own ADC-platform IP and manufactures conjugates for other companies' programs. Appears in this pass as a named 2025 filer on multi-payload ADCs, and separately as a platform vendor publishing its own dual-payload-ADC (dpADC) capability material.

## Role

Two independent 2025 sources place WuXi XDC at the center of the dual-payload trend: [[Cancer Antibody Conjugates Patent Landscape 2025 (industry report)]] and [[ADC Linker Chemistry Technology Landscape 2026 (industry report)]] both name a January 2025 WO filing from WuXi XDC Singapore that explicitly addresses multiple-payload ADCs as a strategy against drug resistance and for improved efficacy - though neither vendor report gives the WO publication number, so this pass could not pull the filing's own claim text. Separately, WuXi XDC's own platform material (its "WuXiTecan" payload series and "WuXiDARx" DAR-control technology) describes a WuXiTecan-2-based dual-payload (MMAE + WuXiTecan-2) ADC with enhanced anti-tumor efficacy in a CDX preclinical model, and states that in 2025 the group explored over 5,600 molecules across bispecific ADCs, dual-payload ADCs, degrader-antibody conjugates, antibody-oligonucleotide conjugates, antibody-peptide conjugates, and LYTACs.

## What it constrains

As a CRDMO, WuXi XDC's own claims are simultaneously a filer's IP position and a vendor's capability pitch - the "5,600 molecules explored" and preclinical dpADC figures come from the company's own promotional material, not from an independent trial or an examined patent claim, and should be weighted accordingly against the vendor-report corroboration of its actual WO filing.
