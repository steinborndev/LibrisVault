# SystImmune

Biotech developer of izalontamab brengitecan (BL-B01D1, "iza-bren"), the first bispecific antibody-drug conjugate to reach approval worldwide. Partnered with Bristol Myers Squibb for development and commercialisation outside China.

## Role

BL-B01D1 is the reference construct this pass uses throughout [[Bispecific Antibody-Drug Conjugate]]: a tetravalent EGFR x HER3 bispecific IgG1 antibody, conjugated via a cathepsin-B-cleavable linker to Ed-04 (a camptothecin-derivative topoisomerase I inhibitor) at DAR 8. It is dual-target but single-payload - the vault's baseline example for distinguishing the dual-target axis from the dual-payload axis, since press coverage of "next-generation ADCs" frequently elides the two. It met its primary endpoint in a global Phase III trial for recurrent/metastatic nasopharyngeal carcinoma, reported 2025-07-03, and holds an FDA Breakthrough Therapy Designation for EGFR-mutated NSCLC; it is also in trials for oesophageal squamous cell carcinoma, small-cell lung cancer, and breast cancer.

## What it constrains

No composition-of-matter patent record for BL-B01D1 itself was pulled directly in this pass (background came from clinical-trial reports and drug-profile summaries, not a Google Patents / USPTO record) - so while BL-B01D1's clinical and regulatory status is well corroborated across multiple independent sources, this vault does not yet hold direct evidence of its own patent claim scope or priority date.
