# Antibody-Drug Conjugates Having a Tailor-Made Drug-to-Antibody Ratio (patent)

WO2025093562A1, applicant BioNTech SE. Priority date 2023-10-30, filed 2024-10-29, published 2025-05-08. Status: pending (no grant found in this pass). Read via the Google Patents record, not the original PDF.

## Summary

Claims an engineered IgG hinge region carrying a predetermined number of cysteine residues, using heterologous hinge-Fc pairings (mismatched immunoglobulin isotypes) so that conjugation sites are set by the engineered hinge rather than by the antibody's native structure. All hinge cysteines are claimed to form stable inter-chain disulfide bonds before conjugation, and the claim specifies avoiding cysteine insertion into conserved regions to limit immunogenicity risk. Because DAR follows directly from hinge cysteine count (roughly double the count), the method claims a DAR range from about 2 to about 22, with DAR 4-8 highlighted as the target for homogeneous product.

- Supports [[Site-Specific DAR Engineering]]: the sole evidence in this pass for controlling DAR by antibody-hinge engineering rather than by conjugation-reaction control or linker architecture.
