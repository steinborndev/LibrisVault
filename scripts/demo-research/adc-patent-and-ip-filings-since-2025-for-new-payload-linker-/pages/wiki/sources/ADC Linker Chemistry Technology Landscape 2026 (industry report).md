# ADC Linker Chemistry Technology Landscape 2026 (industry report)

A PatSnap Eureka R&D blog post, "ADC Linker Chemistry Technology Landscape 2026." A patent-analytics vendor's blog summarising a database query over linker-chemistry patent filings, spanning 2013-2026 records; not a primary patent document, not peer reviewed - the same class of source as [[Cancer Antibody Conjugates Patent Landscape 2025 (industry report)]] and the vault's existing [[Adaptive Optics Patent Landscape (industry report)]].

## Summary

Frames linker-chemistry filing activity in three phases. 2013-2016 ("Foundational"): 24 of 34 disclosed clinical ADCs used cysteine-thiol linkages. 2016-2020 ("Diversification"): Daiichi Sankyo's hydrophilic-linker filings (2014-2018, US/EP) and RemeGen's filings (2017-2018) establish hydrophilic linker design as a differentiator. 2021-2026 ("Maturation"): filings cluster heavily in 2024-2026 and are predominantly from Chinese assignees; by jurisdiction breadth, Daiichi Sankyo, RemeGen, and Multitude Therapeutics each file across 6 jurisdictions, Shanghai Junshi Biosciences across 5, Lepu Biopharma across 4, Byondis B.V. across 3.

Four linker-chemistry clusters are named: enzyme-sensitive peptide-cleavable (the VC-PABC architecture, still dominant), hydrophilic linkers (PEG-like ethylene glycol units), site-specific conjugation (AJICAP 2G, allenamide-based chemistries), and novel metal-organic linkers (Pt(II) Lx(R) technology). No per-cluster claim counts are given.

On dual/multi-payload and hydrophilicity-as-baseline specifically, the report names: WuXi XDC Singapore's January 2025 WO filing (multiple-payload ADCs); PinotBio's 2025 AU/EP filings (camptothecin-based, super-toxin, and antiapoptotic-inhibitor combinations); CSPC Megalith's 2025 AU filings (hydrophilic linker design as the primary claim, read by the report as a sign hydrophilicity has moved from differentiator to baseline expectation); and Otsuka Pharmaceutical's 2025 IN/EP filings (linkers bearing COOH, SO3H, or PO3H2 groups).

## Extracted claims

- Supports [[Multidrug Branched Linker]]: corroborates hydrophilic substitution as a repeated 2025 claiming pattern across multiple assignees (CSPC Megalith, Otsuka), not just the one filing ([[Multidrug Linker and Antibody-Drug Conjugate (patent)]]) this pass could read in full.
- Supports [[Site-Specific DAR Engineering]] only indirectly: this report's site-specific-conjugation cluster (AJICAP 2G, allenamide chemistries) addresses site-specificity through conjugation chemistry, a different mechanism from [[Antibody-Drug Conjugates Having a Tailor-Made Drug-to-Antibody Ratio (patent)]]'s hinge-engineering approach - named here as contrast, not corroboration.

## Caveats

- Vendor blog content, not a formal patent-analytics report with a methodology section; figures could not be cross-checked against USPTO/EPO/CNIPA totals directly.
- The WuXi XDC, PinotBio, CSPC Megalith, and Otsuka filings are each named only in summary form (assignee, rough date, jurisdiction, one-line claim description) - none of their WO/patent numbers were given, so this pass could not pull the underlying claim text to verify these descriptions independently.
- "2013-2026" as a stated coverage window includes 2026 filings not yet a full year old at the time this report was read; recent-year counts likely undercount true filing volume due to publication lag, as the companion [[Cancer Antibody Conjugates Patent Landscape 2025 (industry report)]] states explicitly for its own recent-year figures.
