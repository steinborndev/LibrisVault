# Dual-Payload Antibody Drug Conjugates (patent)

WO2025080711A1, applicant Sutro Biopharma Inc. Priority date 2023-10-13, filed 2024-10-09, published 2025-04-17. Status: pending (no grant found in this pass). Read via the Google Patents record, not the original PDF.

## Summary

Claims an ADC bearing two distinct payload classes on one antibody, each attached via its own linker: at least one topoisomerase I inhibitor (camptothecin-derivative class - irinotecan, SN-38, topotecan, exatecan named) via a first linker, and at least one DNA-damage-response (DDR) inhibitor (PARP inhibitors olaparib/talazoparib/rucaparib, CHK1/CHK2 inhibitors, or ATR inhibitors) via a second, independent linker. Both linkers can independently be protease-cleavable, enzyme-cleavable, pH-sensitive, or non-cleavable.

The stated rationale is synthetic lethality: topoisomerase inhibition damages DNA, but a cell can survive that damage by activating its DNA-damage-response pathway; co-delivering a DDR inhibitor through the same conjugate disables that escape route in the same cell, at the same time, without relying on systemic co-administration of two separate drugs.
