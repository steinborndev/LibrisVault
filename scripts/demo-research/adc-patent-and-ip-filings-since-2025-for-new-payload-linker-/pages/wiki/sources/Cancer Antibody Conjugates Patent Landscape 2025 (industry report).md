# Cancer Antibody Conjugates Patent Landscape 2025 (industry report)

Knowmade's "Cancer & Antibody Conjugates Patent Landscape Analysis 2025," announced via GlobeNewswire press releases (one dated 2026-02-19, a follow-up dated 2026-08-17). A patent-analytics vendor's commercial report over a database query, not a primary patent document or a peer-reviewed source - read as a directional aggregate, the same caveat that applies to the vault's existing [[Adaptive Optics Patent Landscape (industry report)]] from an unrelated domain.

## Summary

Scope: 3,761 patent families in the cancer-antibody-conjugate space. Annual filings more than doubled between 2018 and 2024, approaching 500 new patent families a year, driven by topoisomerase-I-inhibitor payloads (the deruxtecan and govitecan families specifically named), new linker and site-specific conjugation technology, and expansion of ADC programs from hematologic cancers into solid tumors. The report segments the landscape along antibody target-cancer-type, linker type (cleavable vs. uncleavable), and payload type (small-molecule drug vs. radioisotope).

Named leading assignees: Roche/Genentech, Daiichi Sankyo, Gilead Sciences, AstraZeneca, Pfizer, Hengrui, Novartis, Bristol Myers Squibb, AbbVie - individual filing counts per assignee were not disclosed in the public summary. 147 "IP newcomer" organisations were identified (first-time filers in this space), 76 of them based in China, which the report reads as evidence of China's rising role in ADC research and manufacturing, not just clinical development. Clinically: roughly 15 ADCs approved worldwide, 200+ in clinical trials, 400+ reported in earlier-stage development.

On multi-payload filings specifically, the report names WuXi XDC Singapore's January 2025 WO filing as explicitly addressing multiple-payload ADCs as a resistance/efficacy strategy, and PinotBio's 2025 AU and EP filings as claiming combinations of camptothecin-based, "super-toxin," and antiapoptotic-inhibitor payload-linker conjugates on one antibody.

## Caveats

- Commercial patent-analytics report; the underlying database query logic and family-counting methodology were not independently checked.
- Individual assignee filing counts, and the WuXi XDC / PinotBio filing numbers themselves, were not recovered in this pass - only the press-release-level summary was reachable, not the full report.
- The 2026-02-19 and 2026-08-17 press releases appear to describe the same underlying report; treated here as one source rather than two, since no substantive difference between them was found.
