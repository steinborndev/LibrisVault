# Binding Molecule and Antibody-Drug Conjugate (patent)

WO2025108427A1, applicants Velavigo (Shanghai) Ltd and Velavigo Bio Inc. Priority date 2023-11-23, filed 2024-11-22, published 2025-05-30. Status: pending (no grant found in this pass). Read via the Google Patents record, not the original PDF.

## Summary

Claims immunoglobulin single variable domains (ISVDs - a nanobody-format binder, smaller than a conventional IgG Fab) that bind EGFR and/or cMet, combined into ADCs. The claimed rationale for the smaller binding format is improved tumor-tissue penetration relative to a full-size bispecific IgG. A specific claimed feature is bi-epitope binding to cMet (two distinct binding domains against cMet itself, on top of the EGFR arm), said to enhance receptor-mediated endocytosis and so improve payload internalisation, plus reduced off-target toxicity. Named therapeutic applications: lung, breast, gastric, and head/neck cancers.

## Extracted claims

- Supports [[Bispecific Antibody-Drug Conjugate]]: evidence that 2025 bispecific-ADC filings are pursuing smaller antibody-fragment (ISVD/nanobody) formats as an alternative to full IgG-format bispecifics, with a distinct claimed rationale (tissue penetration, endocytosis enhancement) rather than just antigen coverage.
