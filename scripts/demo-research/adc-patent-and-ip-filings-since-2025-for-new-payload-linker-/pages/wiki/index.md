# Index

Entry point to the wiki.

- **astronomy** - exoplanets, the instruments that find them, and the stars they orbit
- **computing** - distributed systems, storage engines and the data structures under them
- **climate-science** - the carbon cycle, ocean circulation and the proxy record
- **machine-learning** - how models are trained, evaluated and made to behave
- **materials-science** - structure, defects and why materials fail
- **cooking** - technique, fermentation and why recipes work
- **neuroscience** - neurons, circuits and the methods used to watch them
- **cryptography** - primitives, protocols and the assumptions under them
- **economics** - markets, money and the way policy moves them
- **photography** - optics, sensors and what a photograph physically is
- **linguistics** - how languages are structured and how they change
- **knowledge-management** - how the wiki itself is supposed to work
- **music-theory** - harmony, rhythm and tuning
- **maritime-history** - navigation, trade routes and the ships that ran them
- **cartography** - projections, generalisation and what a map decides to omit
- **typography** - type, spacing and legibility
- **mycology** - fungi, their fruiting bodies and their substrates
- **oncology** - targeted cancer therapeutics, their resistance mechanisms, and the patent landscape around them
