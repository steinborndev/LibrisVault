Case length follows the page count with a floor of two tiles and a cap of five; a department that outgrows its case gets a second one in the next free bay instead of a longer one. Stability. A case keeps its bay for life. Re-shelving is a maintenance action the caretaker performs on screen, never a side effect of a new domain.
Fullness, not length. Spines saturate on a logarithmic curve, so a 1000-page department looks full rather than ten times longer than a 100-page one.
